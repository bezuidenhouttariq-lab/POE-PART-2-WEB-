Get Involved

Can’t adopt right now? Volunteer, foster or sponsor

We rely on volunteers and monthly sponsors to keep the shelter running. Pick a way to help below.

Volunteer with us

A few hours a week makes a real difference. Tell us when you’re free and we’ll match you to a role.

Dog walking & enrichment

Walk, play with, and socialise dogs waiting for adoption. No experience necessary — training provided.

Admin & events

Help with adoption paperwork, social media, or our monthly open shelter day.

Transport & vet runs

Drive animals to vet appointments and adoption events. A reliable car and a free morning are all you need.

Open your home as a foster carer

Foster care gives an animal a break from the shelter and frees up a kennel for the next rescue. Stays usually last two to eight weeks.

What we provide

Food, bedding, toys, and all vet care. You provide the love and a safe space.

What we ask

A quiet corner, daily company, and a weekly check-in with our volunteer coordinator.

Ready to start?

Ask about fostering and we’ll call you within two working days.

Adoption questions, answered

Adoption fees range from R600 for seniors to R1 500 for puppies. Every animal is sterilised, vaccinated, dewormed and microchipped before they go home, so the fee is a fraction of what those treatments would cost you.
