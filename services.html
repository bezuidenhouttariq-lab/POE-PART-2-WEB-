How It Works

Our adoption process

Four straightforward steps take you from browsing to bringing your new companion home.

Browse & enquire

Meet the animals below and submit an enquiry for the one who catches your eye.

Meet & greet

We'll arrange a supervised visit at the shelter so you and the animal can meet in person.

Home check

A quick, friendly home visit or call confirms your space suits the animal's needs.

Take them home

Sign the adoption agreement, collect a starter pack, and welcome them in.

Get Involved

Volunteer with us

We rely entirely on volunteers to keep the shelter running. A few hours a week makes a real difference.

Dog walking & enrichment

Walk, play with, and socialise dogs waiting for adoption. No experience necessary — training provided.

Foster care

Open your home temporarily to an animal that needs space away from the shelter environment.

Admin & events

Help with adoption paperwork, social media, or our monthly open shelter day.

Sponsor an Animal

Give monthly, change a life

Can't adopt right now? A monthly sponsorship covers food, vet care, and shelter costs for a specific animal.

Bronze

R100 / month

Covers a week of food for one animal, plus a photo update every quarter.

Silver

R250 / month

Covers food and basic vet care, plus a monthly photo and progress update.

Gold

R500 / month

Covers full care for one animal, plus a personal visit invitation each quarter.
