Johannesburg South · Non-profit rescue since 2018

Animal rescue in Johannesburg South where every animal gets a soft place to land.

Paws & Purpose rescues, rehabilitates, and rehomes abandoned and abused dogs and cats, and helps our community keep the pets they already love. Run by a small permanent team and a network of dedicated volunteers, we are a no-kill shelter in Rosettenville.
